Era and visibility

All-time and Modern set the draft's era scope. Classic exposes stats during selection, while Hard Mode hides them. Choose those conditions before starting so the information available for each pick is clear.

Team and Decade re-spins

In the normal draft, Team preserves the decade and Decade preserves the team. The allowances reset after a placement. Use the current offer's remaining control deliberately; a spin can still return the same context.

📊What Projected season actually tells you

The eleventh placement automatically produces the normal draft's Projected season. It converts weighted card contributions into a wins/losses projection out of 162 games. The standard calculation is deterministic for its supplied lineup and scoring data, rather than a fresh sequence of 162 random match outcomes.

The scoring panel uses decade ratings, playing-time weighting and a comparison benchmark. Keep those terms with the projection. Card-display wording can describe career statistics while the scoring explanation describes decade contributions, so use the field labels on the screen when recording values.

The target is a projected 162–0. Preserve the eleven entries and the returned pair when judging an attempt. The sport table explains how that notation differs from the NBA, hockey and soccer objectives.
