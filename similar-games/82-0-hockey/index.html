📊A season line is not a per-game average

Skater card labels depend on the chosen basis
BasisCard lineRead it as
Decade AvgG/gm · A/gm · PTS/gmProjected per-game output across the franchise-and-decade sample
Best SeasonG · A · seasonThe supplied goal and assist totals for the selected single season

Decade Avg uses a broader, games-weighted sample. Best Season highlights a supplied season's goals and assists. A larger-looking G number in the second setting is not directly comparable with a G/gm decimal in the first.

The published best-season choice for skaters uses the highest 3 × goals + 2 × assists within the franchise and decade. That selects a season; it is not a replacement for the entire team simulation. If best-season data is missing, a card can fall back to the average line. Read the labels rather than assuming every row changed units.

🧤

Read the goalie separately

SV%
Higher save percentage is favorable.
GAA
Lower goals against average is favorable.

Best Season can supply a goalie season with these same field types. The skater's goals-and-assists rule does not establish which goalie season was selected. Keep both goalie values and the visible season or average context.

↪️Check what the click did

One suitable position type

Selecting the card can place it immediately. The two defense slots fill in sequence. Read the updated six-slot lineup before continuing, especially when the selection completed one of the D jobs.

Several suitable types

A position chooser opens instead. Choose from the usable types offered by that card. This is the moment to consider which opening you spend and which job remains for the next draw.

The solo game starts the season simulation after the sixth placement. The last card therefore leads into the finished record automatically.

🎰Two respins, two preserved conditions

Respin Team
Keep the decade; change the franchise draw.
Respin Decade
Keep the franchise; change its period.

Each is available once per run. If a remaining goalie job makes the current offer awkward, decide which condition you want to preserve before using either control. Check the newly supplied candidates instead of assuming a particular keeper must appear.
