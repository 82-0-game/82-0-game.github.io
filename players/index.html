What happens when a scoring lead meets a steals tradeoff? Read the Bulls and Wizards chapters, the retired-career reference, and two questions for comparing the legal guards.

The whole statistical row

When does breadth matter against a specialist? A completed seven-game Finals sheet puts five kinds of production on the page, with their units and sample clearly attached.

Creation versus finishing

Would you give up some scoring for more passing? Follow the comparison questions, then untangle the famous center story from the positions the game permits.

Three frontcourt jobs

Does a scoring upgrade keep the rebounds and blocks you want? Kareem's center check includes a concrete Finals performance and the measurement gap in his earliest seasons.
