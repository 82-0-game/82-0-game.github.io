Terms: Use Examples with Their Context Intact

Use a comparison for the decision it explains and a real result for the attempt it records. These terms set out the guide’s scope, evidence labels and ownership boundaries.

📖Use the guide to understand a choice

The 82-0 game guide offers interface explanations, worked comparisons and windows for playing browser sports games. Its articles are for reading and personal reference. The guide does not operate the games, register their accounts or certify a season record.

Apply an explanation to the offer you actually receive. Available candidates, compatible placements and permitted actions decide whether an example fits your draft. A useful comparison can help you explain a choice without promising the same season finish.

🏷️Keep the label when you use an example

If you discuss a worked example, retain the conditions that make it meaningful. Removing “illustrative” from a sample turns a teaching aid into a different claim. Likewise, a screenshot of chosen cards without a returned result establishes less than a completed season screen.

🧾Share an attempt with its evidence intact

  1. Check the capture. Review visible account names or personal details before sharing.
  2. Identify the attempt. Keep the actual roster and game-returned record together.
  3. Describe the comparison honestly. A practice setting beside the game is not the score inside it.

The success guide explains the undefeated endpoint, and Close Results helps review an imperfect finish. Those articles interpret a record; they do not approve submissions, authenticate screenshots or act for the game’s leaderboard.

🎮The playing service sets its own conditions

Access and actions

Use a device and connection you are allowed to use. A playing window does not remove a game’s account requirements, network restrictions or device limitations. Follow the controls and conditions shown by the game for its own draft, saving and sign-in features.

Changing screens

The guide describes particular interfaces and decision patterns. A game can change its controls or availability independently. When an offered action differs from an explanation, inspect the current screen instead of forcing a remembered sequence.

🏀Names identify the subject; ownership stays separate

Game titles, NBA and team names, player names, photographs and interface images identify the material being discussed. This independent guide is not endorsed by a league, team or game operator. Reading it does not transfer rights in those underlying materials or grant a separate distribution license.

When reusing a description or illustration, preserve its independent-commentary context and any example label. Do not attribute the guide’s comparison to the game as an official statement. About explains the coverage; Privacy addresses hosting, local controls and game data.
