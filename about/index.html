About 82-0 game

82-0 game is a basketball-themed sports game that has captured attention across social media for its addictive, fast-paced gameplay. The name itself references the perfect regular-season record of 82 wins and zero losses, a dream scenario for any NBA team. Visitors to the site can jump straight into the action, with the classic mode offering a draft experience using full player statistics.

The game is designed to be simple to pick up yet challenging to master, appealing to both casual fans and stat-driven strategists. Beyond the core gameplay, the site fosters an interactive community: players can leave comments on games and rate their experiences, helping others discover the most engaging matchups. All community features are built with privacy in mind—comments store only a display name, message, moderation status, and a one-way visitor hash, while ratings rely on an anonymous first-party cookie and a one-way hash, with no raw IP addresses recorded.

Whether you're chasing that perfect 82-0 season or just looking for a quick basketball fix, 82-0 game offers a fresh, community-driven way to test your coaching and drafting skills.
