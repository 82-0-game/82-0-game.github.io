About Curve Master Football

Curve Master Football puts your aim and creativity to the test. This isn't just about kicking; it's about mastering the swerve. Draw a curved path on the screen to guide the ball around obstacles and into the net, unlocking new challenges as you progress.

Mastering the Perfect Shot

In each level, you'll face walls, defenders, and a goalkeeper. Your task is to draw a curved trajectory that bends around these obstacles and finds the back of the net. The game rewards precision with a Perfect Shot, complete with slow-motion replays when you hit the top corner.

With over 300 levels of increasing difficulty, you'll need to adapt your strategy. Collect coins to unlock more than 30 unique ball skins, and aim for bullseye targets to earn extra points. Daily rewards and a global leaderboard add long-term goals for dedicated players.

PLAY BETTER

Curve Master Football tips

  1. Study the level layout before drawing your path to anticipate obstacles.
  2. Aim for the top corners to trigger a Perfect Shot and slow-motion effect.
  3. Use the curve to bend the ball around defenders rather than trying to power through.
  4. Collect coins in each level to unlock new ball skins.
  5. Hit bullseye targets for extra points to boost your leaderboard rank.
QUICK ANSWERS

Curve Master Football FAQ

How do you control the ball in Curve Master Football?

You draw a curved path on the screen by dragging your finger (mobile) or holding the left mouse button (desktop). Release to shoot the ball along that path.

What is the goal in each level?

Guide the ball past walls, defenders, and the goalkeeper to score a goal. Aim for the top corner for a Perfect Shot and hit bullseye targets for extra points.

How many levels are there and what rewards can I earn?

There are over 300 levels of increasing difficulty. You can collect coins to unlock more than 30 ball skins and claim daily rewards to help your progress.
