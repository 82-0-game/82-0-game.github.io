About GoalShot

GoalShot is a physics-based soccer puzzle game that challenges you to aim and shoot your ball through a variety of obstacles. The goal is simple: hit at least one star mid-flight and then land the ball in the goal to complete each level.

Mastering the Shot

Each level in GoalShot presents a unique layout of walls, boxes, and moving obstacles. You must carefully aim your shot, accounting for the ball's trajectory and the environment. The ball can curve through portals, adding a layer of complexity to your planning.

You have a limited number of balls per level, so every shot counts. As you progress, you'll encounter handcrafted levels that require precise timing and strategic thinking. Unlockable jerseys, hairstyles, balls, and effects provide customization options as you advance.

PLAY BETTER

GoalShot tips

  1. Observe the movement patterns of obstacles before taking your shot.
  2. Use the walls and boxes to your advantage by banking shots off them.
  3. Aim for the stars first, then adjust your trajectory to reach the goal.
  4. Take your time to plan your shot; you have a limited number of balls.
QUICK ANSWERS

GoalShot FAQ

What is the objective in GoalShot?

In GoalShot, you must hit at least one star with your soccer ball mid-flight and then get the ball into the goal to complete the level.

How do I control the ball in GoalShot?

You control the ball by clicking and holding to aim, then releasing to shoot. Adjust the direction and power by moving your mouse while holding.

Are there any limitations on the number of shots I can take?

Yes, each level gives you a limited number of balls. You must plan your shots carefully to complete the level within that limit.
