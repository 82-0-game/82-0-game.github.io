About Extreme Gear Shift Racing

Extreme Gear Shift Racing puts you behind the wheel of a sports car in a 3D racing challenge where the key to victory is nailing every gear change. Race through 11 progressive levels, either solo or against a friend in local two-player mode, and aim to cross the finish line first.

Mastering the Green Zone

The core loop revolves around timing your gear shifts perfectly. As you accelerate, watch for the green zone on the shift indicator and hit the shift button at the right moment to gain maximum acceleration. Miss it, and you'll lose precious speed, allowing opponents to pull ahead.

Beyond shifting, you'll need to drift through marked sections to build up boost, which you can unleash for a sudden surge of speed. With multiple sports cars to choose from and unlock as you progress, each level presents a new test of your timing and racing line. The guided tutorial helps you get started, while desktop keyboard and on-screen mobile controls keep the action accessible.

PLAY BETTER

Extreme Gear Shift Racing tips

  1. Time your gear shifts to land in the green zone for maximum acceleration; even a perfect shift can make the difference in a close race.
  2. Use the handbrake to initiate drifts in marked sections, which fills your boost meter faster than normal cornering.
  3. Save your boost for long straights or the final stretch to overtake opponents when it counts.
  4. In two-player mode, watch your opponent's position and adjust your racing line to block their advances.
QUICK ANSWERS

Extreme Gear Shift Racing FAQ

How does the gear shift mechanic work in Extreme Gear Shift Racing?

You must press the shift button (or key) when the indicator is in the green zone to achieve a perfect gear change, which gives you maximum acceleration. Shifting outside the green zone results in a slower acceleration.

Can I play Extreme Gear Shift Racing with a friend?

Yes, the game features a local two-player mode where you can race against another player on the same device.

What are the controls for Extreme Gear Shift Racing?

On desktop, use W/Up to accelerate, S/Down to brake/reverse, A/D or Left/Right to steer, Space for handbrake, and Shift for boost. On mobile, use the on-screen buttons for shifting, drifting, and boosting.

GUIDEHow to play＋
Start by completing the guided tutorial to learn the basics. When a race begins, use the W or Up Arrow key to accelerate. As the engine revs, watch for the gear shift indicator and press the Shift key or the on-screen Shift button when the marker is in the green zone for a perfect change. Steer with A/D or Left/Right Arrow keys, and use the Space bar for the handbrake when approaching tight corners. When you see a drift section, initiate a drift to fill your boost meter, then press the Shift key or Boost button to activate it for a speed boost.
