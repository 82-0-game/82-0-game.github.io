About Wrestling Revolution Arena

Wrestling Revolution Arena is a 3D wrestling game that combines in-ring competition with backstage management. Step into the boots of a wrestler to take shots in the ring, or switch to a booking career to call the shots and promote entertaining matches for ratings.

Two Careers, One Ring

In wrestling career mode, you focus on your in-ring performance, executing moves and building your reputation with each match. The booking career flips the perspective, putting you in charge of matchmaking and show promotion to keep weekly ratings high. Both paths are available from the start, letting you switch between them freely.

The dual-career structure creates a unique challenge: understanding what makes a match entertaining from a wrestler's perspective helps you book better shows, and vice versa. This cross-training keeps the gameplay fresh and deepens your appreciation for the business. You can play both modes for free, with an optional upgrade to the Pro experience that removes ads and limitations.

Wrestling Revolution Arena tips

Wrestling Revolution Arena FAQ

What is the main objective in Wrestling Revolution Arena?

The main objective depends on the career you choose. In wrestling career, you aim to succeed in matches. In booking career, you promote entertaining shows to boost ratings.

How do I switch between wrestling and booking careers?

You can choose either career at the start, and the game allows you to experience both sides. The exact method to switch is not specified, but both modes are available to play.

Is Wrestling Revolution Arena free to play?

Yes, both wrestling and booking modes are free to play. There is an optional upgrade to the Pro experience that removes ads and limitations.
