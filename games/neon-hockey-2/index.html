Neon Hockey 2

Neon Hockey 2 is a fast-paced arcade air hockey game with neon visuals, offering 1 Player vs AI and 2 Player local multiplayer modes.

About Neon Hockey 2

Neon Hockey 2 brings fast-paced arcade air hockey to your screen with a neon retro aesthetic and smooth physics. Choose to challenge the AI or a friend in local multiplayer. The goal is simple: score 7 goals in a period to win the best-of-3 match.

Mastering the Puck

In Neon Hockey 2, each match is a best-of-3 periods contest. You control a mallet to hit the puck and aim for the opponent's goal. The physics-based gameplay ensures every shot feels responsive, while vibrant visual effects add to the intensity. Customize your mallet size and the maximum puck speed in settings to suit your play style.

The AI in 1 Player mode has been significantly improved, offering three difficulty levels to test your skills. In 2 Player mode, you can compete locally on the same device, making it perfect for quick, competitive sessions. Whether you're honing your reflexes or facing a friend, the game's fast pace keeps you on your toes.

PLAY BETTER

Neon Hockey 2 tips

  1. Adjust mallet size and puck speed in settings to find your preferred balance of control and challenge.
  2. In 1 Player mode, start with a lower AI difficulty to learn the physics, then increase it as you improve.
  3. Use quick, short movements to react to fast shots rather than large sweeps.
  4. In 2 Player mode, anticipate your opponent's moves and position your mallet to block common angles.

Neon Hockey 2 FAQ

How does scoring work in Neon Hockey 2?

You need to score 7 goals to win a period. The match is best of 3 periods, so you must win two periods to win the game.

Can I play Neon Hockey 2 with a friend?

Yes, there is a 2 Players mode that supports local multiplayer on a single device. Player 1 uses the left half of the screen or WASD, and Player 2 uses the right half or arrow keys.

What customization options are available?

You can adjust the mallet size and the maximum puck speed in the settings menu before starting a match.
