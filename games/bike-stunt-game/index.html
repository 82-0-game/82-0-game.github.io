About Bike Stunt Game

Bike Stunt Game is a side-scrolling arcade racer that blends 2D gameplay with 3D depth. Your goal is to complete each level by reaching the finish line while performing tricks and avoiding crashes. With three distinct modes, it offers a fresh challenge every time you play.

Mastering the 2. 5D Perspective

In Bike Stunt Game, your bike moves forward automatically, so your focus is on balance and timing. Use the up and down arrows to lean forward or backward, and hit the spacebar to jump. While airborne, hold down to perform tricks like backflips, which earn style points in Trick Challenge mode.

The game's unique 2. 5D view gives you depth perception for landing perfect jumps, while keeping the simple side-scroller controls. As you master each level, you'll unlock new bikes. With Time Trial, Trick Challenge, and Survival modes, you can test your speed, style, or endurance across a handful of hand-crafted levels.

PLAY BETTER

Bike Stunt Game tips

  1. Time your jumps to land smoothly on ramps and avoid crashes.
  2. In Trick Challenge, hold down longer during jumps to execute more complex tricks and earn higher style points.
  3. In Survival mode, prioritize stability over flashy tricks to avoid crashing.
  4. Use the 2. 5D depth perception to judge distances and land perfectly on loops and obstacles.

Bike Stunt Game FAQ

How does Bike Stunt Game work?

Your bike moves forward automatically. You control balance and perform tricks by leaning, jumping, and holding down during jumps. Complete each level by reaching the finish line.

What are the different modes in Bike Stunt Game?

There are three modes: Time Trial (beat the clock), Trick Challenge (earn style points), and Survival (avoid crashes at all costs).

Can I unlock new bikes in Bike Stunt Game?

Yes, you can unlock new bikes as you master each level.
