Goalpost Shuffle

Goalpost Shuffle is a casual puzzle game where you guide footballs to matching goalposts by clicking the ball and then its facing goalpost.

About Goalpost Shuffle

In Goalpost Shuffle, your task is to send each football to the correct goalpost within the time limit. The game combines casual sports action with puzzle-like precision, as you must click on a ball and then click the goalpost directly facing it. Success depends on quick thinking and accurate clicks.

Mastering the Click Sequence

The core loop is simple: click on a football to select it, then click on the goalpost that is straight ahead. The ball will travel in a straight line to that goalpost. You must repeat this for every ball on the field, ensuring each one reaches its matching goalpost.

The challenge ramps up as you manage multiple balls and goalposts under time pressure. You need to avoid collisions and other issues, so planning your clicks in the right order is crucial. Each level tests your ability to assess the layout quickly and act decisively.

PLAY BETTER

Goalpost Shuffle tips

  1. Scan the field before making your first click to identify which balls are already aligned with their goalposts.
  2. Prioritize balls that have a clear straight path to their goalpost to avoid potential collisions.
  3. Work quickly but accurately – a wrong click can waste valuable time.
  4. If multiple balls are aligned with the same goalpost, send them in an order that avoids overlap.
QUICK ANSWERS

Goalpost Shuffle FAQ

How do I play Goalpost Shuffle?

Click on a football to select it, then click on the goalpost that is directly facing that ball. The ball will travel in a straight line to that goalpost. Repeat for all balls to complete the level within the time limit.

What happens if I click the wrong goalpost?

If you click a goalpost that is not directly facing the selected ball, the ball may not move correctly, potentially causing a collision or other issue. It's best to ensure the goalpost is straight ahead before clicking.

Is there a time limit in Goalpost Shuffle?

Yes, each level has a given time limit. You must send all footballs to their correct goalposts before time runs out to succeed.
