About 3D Mini Curling

3D Mini Curling is a sports simulation that captures the essence of curling on ice. Your goal is to outscore your opponent by sliding stones closer to the target. With careful aim and power adjustment, you can master this satisfying 3D take on a winter classic.

Mastering Stone Delivery

The core loop revolves around taking turns to deliver your stone. You set the direction and power, then release with a click or tap. The stone glides across the ice, and the physics determine its final resting spot. Precision is key, as even a slight misjudgment can leave you short or overshooting the target.

Challenge comes from balancing aggression with caution. You can play solo against the AI or challenge a friend in two-player mode. Each shot requires reading the ice and anticipating your opponent's moves. With practice, you'll learn to control the stone's trajectory and apply the right touch for clutch shots.

PLAY BETTER

3D Mini Curling tips

  1. Take your time to line up the shot; a few extra seconds can improve accuracy.
  2. Start with moderate power to get a feel for the ice friction before attempting stronger throws.
  3. In two-player mode, observe your opponent's stone placement to plan a defensive or offensive shot.
  4. Aim for the center of the target, but consider blocking your opponent's path if they are close.
QUICK ANSWERS

3D Mini Curling FAQ

How do I control the stone in 3D Mini Curling?

You use the mouse or touch to aim and set power, then click or tap to release the stone. The game handles the sliding physics after release.

Can I play 3D Mini Curling with a friend?

Yes, the game supports a two-player mode where you can take turns on the same device, competing to see who can get closest to the target.

What is the objective in 3D Mini Curling?

The objective is to outscore your opponent by sliding your stones closer to the center of the target than theirs. After all stones are played, points are awarded based on proximity.

GUIDEHow to play＋
Start by choosing your game mode: single-player against the computer or local two-player. On your turn, position your aim by moving the mouse or your finger to set the direction. Next, set the power level—too little and the stone won't reach, too much and it may slide past. When ready, click or tap to release the stone. Watch it glide and see where it stops. Alternate turns with your opponent, and after all stones are delivered, the one closest to the center scores points. The player with the highest total wins.
