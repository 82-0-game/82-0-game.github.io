About Placik

Placik puts a fresh spin on football by keeping your player rooted in place. When the ball comes to you, you can direct it in any direction, but your only target is a single goalkeeper who never stops moving. Your goal: find the open space and fire.

Timing Your Shot

In Placik, your player doesn't move, so the core loop revolves around receiving the ball and choosing the right moment to shoot. The goalkeeper is constantly shifting, leaving gaps that open and close quickly. You must read his movement and release the ball when a clear lane appears.

The challenge lies in patience and precision. Since the keeper never stops, every shot is a decision. Wait too long and the window closes; shoot too early and he'll block it. Success comes from observing his pattern and striking when the space is largest.

PLAY BETTER

Placik tips

  1. Watch the goalkeeper's pattern before shooting.
  2. Shoot when he moves to one side, aiming for the opposite corner.
  3. Be patient; don't rush your shot.
  4. If he stays central, aim for the edges.
