About Mini Golf 3D

Mini Golf 3D is a smooth arcade golf game that challenges you to sink the ball in as few strokes as possible. With over 40 progressively harder levels, you'll aim, charge, and strike your way through creative courses while avoiding hazards that can ruin your score.

Mastering the Shot

The core loop is simple: line up your shot, charge your power, and release to send the ball rolling. Each course introduces new angles and obstacles, so you'll need to read the terrain and adjust your aim carefully. Sand traps slow your ball, while water hazards cost you an extra stroke, making precision essential.

As you complete levels, you earn gems that let you unlock new colored balls, adding a layer of progression. The physics feel responsive, so every shot behaves predictably. With difficulty ramping up, you'll need to plan your approach and master the charge mechanic to conquer the toughest holes.

PLAY BETTER

Mini Golf 3D tips

  1. Take your time to line up your shot before charging.
  2. When facing sand traps, add extra power to compensate for the slowdown.
  3. Avoid water hazards entirely; they add a stroke to your score.
  4. Use the environment to your advantage, like banking shots off walls.
  5. Save your gems to unlock balls that suit your style.
QUICK ANSWERS

Mini Golf 3D FAQ

How do I control my shot in Mini Golf 3D?

You use your mouse or finger to aim, then click or tap and hold to charge your power. Release to strike the ball.

What happens if I land in a water hazard?

Landing in water costs you an extra stroke, so it's best to avoid them entirely to keep your score low.

How do I unlock new balls?

You earn gems by completing levels, and you can spend those gems to unlock different colored balls.
