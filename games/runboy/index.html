About Runboy

Runboy is a charming grid-based puzzle game that puts you in the boots of a brave little farmer. Your goal is to navigate an 8×10 tile board, collect scattered seeds, and avoid or confront the mischievous raccoons that invade your fields. Each level is hand-crafted to test your strategic thinking.

Mastering the Tile Board

In Runboy, every move matters. You control the farmer across a fixed 8×10 grid, planning each step to gather seeds while keeping an eye on raccoon positions. The challenge lies in predicting their movements and using the board's layout to your advantage. Each level presents a new spatial puzzle to solve.

As you progress through dozens of hand-crafted levels, the raccoons become more cunning, forcing you to think several steps ahead. The game rewards careful planning and quick decision-making. With only one player, it's a personal test of logic and foresight, perfect for puzzle enthusiasts who enjoy a good mental workout.

PLAY BETTER

Runboy tips

  1. Plan your route before moving to avoid getting cornered by raccoons.
  2. Use the edges of the board to your advantage to limit raccoon approach angles.
  3. When possible, confront raccoons head-on if you can predict their next move.
  4. Prioritize seeds that are in risky areas early, as raccoons may block them later.

Runboy FAQ

How do you play Runboy?

In Runboy, you control a farmer on an 8×10 grid using the WASD keys. Your goal is to collect all seeds while avoiding or confronting raccoons. Each level is a puzzle that requires planning your moves carefully.

What is the objective in Runboy?

The objective is to guide the farmer across the tile board to collect scattered seeds while dealing with raccoons. You must either avoid them or confront them strategically to complete each level.

Is Runboy a single-player game?

Yes, Runboy is a single-player puzzle game. It is tagged as '1 Player', so you play alone, testing your strategic thinking across dozens of hand-crafted levels.
