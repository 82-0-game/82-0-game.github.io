About Ball Chain Blast

Ball Chain Blast puts a sweet spin on sports puzzles. Your goal is to draw chains that connect matching sports balls—football, basketball, tennis, volleyball, and more—then release to blast them. Longer chains mean bigger scores, and chaining eight or more triggers legendary combos with epic multipliers.

Mastering Chain Reactions

The core loop is simple: spot matching balls, draw a chain through them, and let go to blast. But timing and planning matter. You'll need to decide whether to go for a safe short chain or risk a longer one for a higher multiplier. Special balls like bombs, rainbows, and multipliers add layers of strategy, letting you clear tough spots or boost your score.

As you progress through 100 levels, you'll face five hilarious boss opponents that require clever chain tactics to defeat. Three difficulty modes let you adjust the challenge, and your progress saves automatically. With power-ups to collect and a variety of sports balls, every level offers a fresh puzzle to solve.

PLAY BETTER

Ball Chain Blast tips

  1. Prioritize chaining 8 or more balls to activate legendary combos and massive score multipliers.
  2. Use rainbow balls to substitute for any sports ball, making it easier to create long chains.
  3. Save bomb balls for clusters of frozen balls to clear them efficiently.
  4. When facing bosses, look for patterns in their defenses and use multipliers to maximize damage.
  5. Experiment with different chain paths to discover hidden bonus opportunities.

Ball Chain Blast FAQ

How do I play Ball Chain Blast?

Draw a chain that connects matching sports balls on the board, then release to blast them. Longer chains yield higher scores and can trigger special effects.

What are the special balls in Ball Chain Blast?

There are five special balls: bombs, rainbows, multipliers, frozen balls, and mega balls. Each has a unique effect, such as clearing a large area or boosting your score.

How many levels are in Ball Chain Blast?

Ball Chain Blast features 100 levels, each with its own puzzle layout. You'll also encounter five boss opponents along the way.

GUIDEHow to play＋
Start by looking at the board and identifying groups of at least two matching sports balls. Use your mouse or finger to draw a line that connects them, making sure the chain doesn't cross itself or include mismatched balls. When you're ready, lift your input to blast the chain. Aim for longer chains to earn more points and trigger special effects. Keep an eye out for special balls and use them wisely to clear obstacles or defeat bosses.
