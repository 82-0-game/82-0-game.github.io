About English Checkers

English Checkers, also known as Draughts, puts your tactical thinking to the test in turn-based PvP battles. The goal is simple: capture all your opponent's pieces or block their moves. With a rating system and detailed stats, every match contributes to your growth as a strategist.

Mastering the Board

Each turn, you click a checker and then a target cell to move diagonally forward. Captures are mandatory when available, and you can chain multiple jumps in one turn. Planning ahead is crucial, as a single misstep can leave your pieces vulnerable to a counterattack.

Beyond the basic rules, English Checkers rewards foresight and positional play. Your personal profile tracks wins, losses, and capture percentage, while the rating system lets you measure progress against opponents of varying skill. Use the built-in chat to discuss strategies and find worthy rivals.

PLAY BETTER

English Checkers tips

  1. Control the center of the board to limit your opponent's options.
  2. Keep your back row intact as long as possible to prevent enemy pieces from becoming kings.
  3. Sacrifice a piece if it forces a double capture that gives you a material advantage.
  4. Watch for forced capture sequences and plan your moves to avoid leaving pieces en prise.

English Checkers FAQ

How do I move a checker in English Checkers?

Click or tap a checker to select it, then click or tap an empty target cell diagonally forward. If a capture is possible, you must take it.

What happens when a checker reaches the last row?

In standard draughts rules, a checker that reaches the last row becomes a king, which can move and capture diagonally in both directions. This game follows those rules.

Can I play against another person online?

Yes, English Checkers is a multiplayer PvP game. You can find opponents through matchmaking or use the built-in chat to arrange games with friends.

GUIDEHow to play＋
Start by selecting a game from the menu. You'll be matched with an opponent or can invite a friend. On your turn, click one of your checkers to highlight it, then click an empty target cell diagonally forward to move. If a jump is possible, you must take it. After capturing, you may continue jumping if another capture is available. The game ends when you capture all enemy pieces or block them from moving.
