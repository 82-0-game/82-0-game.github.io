About BuzzBall

BuzzBall is a casual clicker game with a quirky twist: bees carry footballs and drop them one by one. Your job is to catch each ball in a glove to rack up points. It's a simple yet challenging test of timing and reflexes.

Mastering the Catch

In BuzzBall, the core loop is straightforward: watch as bees hover overhead and release footballs at their own pace. Click at the right moment to position your glove and snag the ball. Each successful catch adds to your score, and the rhythm of the drops keeps you on your toes.

The challenge ramps up because you have limited chances to miss. Every ball that hits the ground without being caught counts against you. This pressure forces you to focus and react quickly, making each catch satisfying and each miss costly. It's a game of precision and patience.

PLAY BETTER

BuzzBall tips

  1. Watch the bee's movement to predict when it will drop the ball.
  2. Click just as the ball is about to reach your glove for a clean catch.
  3. Stay calm and avoid clicking too early, as timing is key.
  4. Focus on one ball at a time to avoid missing due to distraction.

BuzzBall FAQ

What is BuzzBall?

BuzzBall is a casual clicker game where bees drop footballs and you catch them in a glove to score points. You have limited chances to miss, so timing is crucial.

How do I control the glove in BuzzBall?

You control the glove by clicking. Click at the right moment to position the glove and catch the falling football.

What happens if I miss a ball in BuzzBall?

Missing a ball counts as a miss. You have a limited number of misses, and when you run out, the game ends.
