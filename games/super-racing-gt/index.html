About Super Racing GT

Super Racing GT is a high-stakes racing game where you buy and tune cars to dominate the competition. Enter the arena as a heroic racer, face off against numerous opponents from different clans, and defeat each series' big boss to claim glory.

Outsmarting Rival Clans

The core loop of Super Racing GT revolves around earning money from races, then investing it in faster cars and performance upgrades. Each victory brings you closer to the next clan's territory, where tougher racers and unique challenges await. Your tuned machine is your ticket to survival.

Rival clans won't show mercy, and their bosses are especially ruthless, often resorting to aggressive tactics to blast you off the road. You must balance aggressive driving with smart car choices and upgrades to outlast the competition. Progress through series after series, proving you have the skill and nerve to be the best.

PLAY BETTER

Super Racing GT tips

  1. Save your money for a high-performance car early on—it pays off in later races.
  2. Upgrade your car's acceleration and top speed to outrun aggressive rivals.
  3. Learn each clan's racing style; some may try to block you, so stay alert.
  4. Don't be afraid to use aggressive tactics against bosses—they won't hold back.
  5. Practice on each track to find the best racing lines and overtaking spots.
QUICK ANSWERS

Super Racing GT FAQ

How do I unlock new races in Super Racing GT?

You progress by defeating the big boss of each clan series. Win races to earn money, upgrade your car, and take on the boss to advance to the next series.

What is the main objective in Super Racing GT?

Your goal is to win races against rival clans and their bosses, using your earnings to buy and tune cars for better performance. Beat all opponents to become the top racer.

Are there different types of cars to buy in Super Racing GT?

Yes, you can purchase various cars using race winnings. Each car has different performance stats, so choose one that fits your driving style and upgrade it to stay competitive.
