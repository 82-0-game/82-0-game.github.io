About GapShot

GapShot is a casual soccer clicker that puts your aim to the test. Your goal is simple: make the soccer ball pass between the legs of the goalkeeper. With limited failures allowed, each shot counts as you try to score without hitting the keeper or sending the ball out of bounds.

Mastering the Nutmeg

In GapShot, the core loop revolves around timing and precision. You click or tap to send the ball toward the goalkeeper, aiming for the narrow gap between his legs. The ball must travel cleanly through that space to score. If it hits the keeper's legs or goes outside the target area, it counts as a failure.

The challenge intensifies because you have a limited number of failures before the game ends. This forces you to stay focused and adjust your aim with each attempt. As you play, you'll learn to read the goalkeeper's stance and find the best moment to strike, making every successful nutmeg satisfying.

PLAY BETTER

GapShot tips

  1. Focus on the center of the gap between the keeper's legs for a clean shot.
  2. Wait for the goalkeeper to be still before shooting to improve accuracy.
  3. Avoid rushing; a steady aim is better than a quick, wild shot.
  4. If you miss, adjust your aim slightly rather than making big changes.
QUICK ANSWERS

GapShot FAQ

What is the objective in GapShot?

The objective is to make the soccer ball pass between the legs of the goalkeeper. Each successful nutmeg scores, but hitting the legs or sending the ball out of bounds counts as a failure.

How many failures are allowed in GapShot?

The game allows only a limited number of failures. Once you exceed that limit, the game ends, so you need to be precise with your shots.

What happens if the ball hits the goalkeeper's legs?

If the ball hits the goalkeeper's legs or goes outside the target area, it is counted as a failure. You have a limited number of failures, so avoid these mistakes to keep playing.
