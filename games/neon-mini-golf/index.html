About Neon Mini Golf

Neon Mini Golf brings a synthwave glow to classic mini-golf. With 39 handcrafted levels, realistic physics, and a sleek neon aesthetic, you'll drag, aim, and release to sink the ball. Bumpers, walls, and teleporters add a twist to every shot.

Mastering the Neon Course

In Neon Mini Golf, each level presents a fresh puzzle. You drag to set your aim, then release to send the ball rolling. The physics feel true, so judging power and angle matters. Watch for obstacles that can redirect your ball or send it somewhere unexpected.

As you progress, the challenge ramps up. Earning stars on each hole unlocks the next set of levels. The neon world is visually striking, but precision is key. Whether on desktop or mobile, the controls stay smooth, letting you focus on perfecting each shot.

PLAY BETTER

Neon Mini Golf tips

  1. Take your time to line up shots; a moment of planning can save strokes.
  2. Use walls to bank shots around obstacles and reach tricky angles.
  3. Watch for teleporters and plan your shot to use them to your advantage.
  4. Adjust power carefully—too much can send you past the hole.
  5. Aim for the center of the hole to avoid lip-outs.
QUICK ANSWERS

Neon Mini Golf FAQ

How do I control the ball in Neon Mini Golf?

Click or tap on the ball, drag to set your aim and power, then release to shoot. The game works with mouse on desktop and touch on mobile.

What is the goal in Neon Mini Golf?

The goal is to sink the ball into the hole in as few strokes as possible. Each level has obstacles like bumpers, walls, and teleporters that affect your shot.

How do I unlock new levels in Neon Mini Golf?

You earn stars based on your performance on each hole. Collect enough stars to unlock the next set of levels. There are 39 handcrafted levels in total.
