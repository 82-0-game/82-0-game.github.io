About Real Advanced Monster Truck Simulator

Real Advanced Monster Truck Simulator is a stunt and destruction driving game where you take control of a monstrous truck. Your main objective is to perform crazy race stunts and cause destruction with your derby car, smashing through a realistic 3D environment and demolishing everything in your way.

Mastering the Mayhem

In Real Advanced Monster Truck Simulator, the core gameplay loop revolves around driving your truck through a detailed 3D world, using the environment as your playground. You'll accelerate, steer, and jump over obstacles, all while aiming to cause as much destruction as possible. The realistic damage system means every collision leaves visible marks, making each run a unique spectacle of twisted metal and flying debris.

The challenge lies in balancing reckless driving with control. While the goal is to smash and crash, you'll need to navigate carefully to avoid getting stuck or losing momentum. As you progress, you'll learn to read the terrain, time your jumps, and use the truck's weight to your advantage. The highscore system encourages you to push your limits and find the most spectacular ways to cause chaos.

PLAY BETTER

Real Advanced Monster Truck Simulator tips

  1. Use ramps to launch your truck into the air and perform stunts for style points.
  2. Target clusters of objects to cause more destruction in a single pass.
  3. Maintain momentum by avoiding sharp turns at high speed, as you might lose control.
  4. Experiment with different angles of approach to find the most spectacular crashes.

Real Advanced Monster Truck Simulator FAQ

What is the main objective in Real Advanced Monster Truck Simulator?

The main objective is to perform crazy race stunts and cause destruction with your monster truck. You drive through a realistic 3D environment, smashing and demolishing everything in your path.

How do I control the monster truck?

You control the truck using the W, A, S, D keys. W accelerates, S brakes or reverses, and A/D steer left and right.

Is there a scoring system in the game?

Yes, the game features a highscore system. Your performance in stunts and destruction contributes to your score, encouraging you to be more reckless and creative.
