Drive and Avoid!

Drive and Avoid! is a 2D reflex game where you steer a blue car through 30 levels, dodging traffic and orbs to survive.

About Drive and Avoid!

In Drive and Avoid!, you take the wheel of a blue car on a mission to survive each level. The core objective is simple: dodge oncoming cars and evade the spheres fired by an evil orb. With 30 levels of increasing difficulty, your reflexes and quick thinking are your best tools for staying on the road.

Mastering Evasive Driving

The gameplay loop in Drive and Avoid! is straightforward yet demanding. You control your blue car on a 2D plane, moving left or right to steer clear of obstacles. Other cars appear as moving hazards, while the evil orb launches spheres at you. Each successful dodge keeps your health intact, but one mistake can cost you dearly.

As you progress through the 30 levels, the challenge ramps up. The first level eases you in, but by the final stage, you'll need sharp reflexes and strategic positioning. Your health is your lifeline—if it runs out, you restart your journey. This high-stakes design keeps every moment tense and rewarding.

PLAY BETTER

Drive and Avoid! tips

  1. Stay alert and watch for the evil orb's firing pattern to anticipate sphere trajectories.
  2. Prioritize dodging spheres over cars, as they may come from unexpected angles.
  3. Use short, precise movements to avoid overcorrecting into another hazard.
  4. Learn each level's rhythm—obstacles often appear in predictable sequences.
  5. Keep your car near the center for more reaction time on both sides.

Drive and Avoid! FAQ

How do I control the car in Drive and Avoid!?

You can move your blue car left or right using the arrow keys on your keyboard, or by clicking the left and right mouse buttons.

What happens if my health runs out?

If your health reaches zero, you'll need to restart the level. Your health decreases when you collide with other cars or get hit by the evil orb's spheres.

How many levels are there in Drive and Avoid!?

There are 30 levels in total. The first level is the easiest, and the difficulty increases as you progress, with the last level being quite challenging.
