About MasterDash

MasterDash drops you onto a city skyline where the goal is simple: keep moving forward. You leap from rooftop to rooftop, dashing and dodging obstacles that block your path. With each jump, you push your speed to the limit, feeling the rush of high-altitude parkour.

Reading the Rooftop Gaps

In MasterDash, your fingers control every leap and dash. You must time your jumps to clear the gaps between scattered rooftops, while also dodging obstacles that appear in your path. The core loop is fast: react quickly, adjust your trajectory mid-air, and land cleanly to keep your momentum.

As you progress, the rooftops become more challenging, demanding sharper reflexes and better timing. Each successful dash builds your speed, but a single mistimed jump can end your run. The challenge lies in balancing aggression with precision, pushing your limits without losing control.

PLAY BETTER

MasterDash tips

  1. Time your jumps early to clear wide gaps.
  2. Watch for obstacles and adjust your jump timing to dodge them.
  3. Keep your rhythm steady to maintain speed.
  4. Anticipate the next rooftop's position before you land.

MasterDash FAQ

How do you play MasterDash?

Click or tap to make your character jump from rooftop to rooftop. Time your jumps to clear gaps and dodge obstacles while maintaining speed.

What is the objective in MasterDash?

The objective is to keep moving forward by leaping between rooftops, avoiding obstacles, and pushing your speed as far as you can.

What controls does MasterDash use?

MasterDash uses simple controls: mouse click or tap on the screen to jump. There are no other buttons or keys needed.
