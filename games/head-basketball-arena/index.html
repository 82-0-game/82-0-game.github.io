Head Basketball Arena

Head Basketball Arena is a simplified basketball game where you pick a character, customize the match, and score with kicks or headers.

About Head Basketball Arena

Head Basketball Arena strips basketball down to its essentials. Before the match, you choose your character and tweak the court, weather, AI difficulty, and match length. Then, with just four buttons, you move, jump, and strike the ball to outscore your opponent.

Mastering the Four-Button Controls

The core loop is straightforward: control your character with two movement keys, jump to contest the ball, and use the kick button to send it toward the goal with your foot or head. Each possession is a quick duel of positioning and timing, as you try to find an open angle before your rival can react.

Because you can adjust AI difficulty and match duration before tip-off, every game can feel different. Shorter matches reward aggressive play, while longer ones test your consistency. The weather and playground choices add visual variety, but your ability to read the ball's bounce and time your kicks will decide who wins.

PLAY BETTER

Head Basketball Arena tips

  1. Time your jump to meet the ball at its highest point for a more powerful header.
  2. Use the kick button while moving to add direction to your shot.
  3. Watch your opponent's positioning and aim for the opposite corner of the goal.
  4. Adjust AI difficulty down if you're learning the timing, then ramp it up for a real test.
QUICK ANSWERS

Head Basketball Arena FAQ

How do I control my character in Head Basketball Arena?

You use two keys to move left and right, one key to jump, and one key to kick the ball with your foot or head. All menu selections are made with the mouse.

Can I customize the match before playing?

Yes, before each game you can choose your character and customize its appearance, as well as the playground, weather, AI difficulty, and match duration.

What is the objective in Head Basketball Arena?

The objective is to score more points than your opponent by kicking the ball into the goal using your foot or head, within the set match duration.
