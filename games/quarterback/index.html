About Quarterback

Quarterback is a football-themed skill game where your throwing arm is the star. The core objective is to complete a series of challenges by swiping to throw the ball accurately at a range of targets, from receivers to tires and even mascots, testing your precision and touch.

Reading the Target

Each challenge presents a different target, and your swipe determines the trajectory and power of the throw. You'll need to judge the distance, account for any obstacles, and time your swipe to release the ball at the right moment. Success comes from reading the situation quickly and executing a clean, accurate pass.

As you progress, the challenges become more varied and demanding, requiring you to adapt your throwing style to hit unconventional targets. The game rewards practice and a steady hand, offering a satisfying test of skill that keeps you coming back for one more throw.

PLAY BETTER

Quarterback tips

  1. Use a quick, short swipe for close targets and a longer, slower swipe for distance.
  2. Watch the target's movement and lead your throw if it's moving.
  3. Aim for the center of the target for a more reliable hit.
  4. Experiment with different swipe angles to find the best trajectory for each challenge.

Quarterback FAQ

How do I throw the ball in Quarterback?

You throw by swiping on the screen. The direction and speed of your swipe determine the ball's path and power.

What kinds of targets will I aim?

You'll aim at a variety of targets, including receivers, tires, and mascots, each requiring a different approach.

Is Quarterback about accuracy or power?

It's about both. You need to judge the right power and angle for each target, making precision and control key.
