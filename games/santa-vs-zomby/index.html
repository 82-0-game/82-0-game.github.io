About Santa Vs Zomby

Santa Vs Zomby puts you in charge of Santa Claus as he faces a zombie invasion on Christmas Eve. The undead have raided his base and made off with all the presents. Your mission is to launch Santa at the zombies, using physics to topple them and clear each level to save Christmas.

Mastering the Launch

The core gameplay loop is straightforward: aim, launch Santa, and watch the physics do the rest. You'll need to account for trajectory and momentum to send Santa crashing into zombie clusters. Each successful hit can cause chain reactions, knocking down multiple enemies at once and earning you progress toward clearing the level.

As you advance, levels introduce trickier arrangements of zombies and obstacles, demanding careful planning. You'll decide the best angle and power for each shot, sometimes targeting weak points to maximize destruction. With simple rules and a clear goal, the challenge lies in mastering the physics to recover every gift and complete each stage.

PLAY BETTER

Santa Vs Zomby tips

  1. Aim for zombies that are close together to trigger chain reactions.
  2. Use the environment to your advantage, such as causing objects to fall on zombies.
  3. Adjust your launch power based on the distance and obstacles between Santa and the zombies.
  4. Observe the physics of each level before launching to plan your first shot.
  5. Prioritize clearing zombies that are positioned to block access to others.
QUICK ANSWERS

Santa Vs Zomby FAQ

What is the goal in Santa Vs Zomby?

The goal is to launch Santa Claus at zombies to knock them down and clear every level, recovering the stolen Christmas gifts.

How do you control Santa in Santa Vs Zomby?

You control Santa by using the mouse or tapping on the screen. Click or tap to aim and release to launch him at the zombies.

What happens when you clear a level in Santa Vs Zomby?

When you clear a level by defeating all zombies, you progress to the next stage, which presents new arrangements and challenges.
