About SCARS

SCARS drops you into the driver's seat of a compact turbo city car for fast-paced 2D arcade racing. The goal is simple: outpace opponents across urban streets, night circuits, and futuristic tracks. With sharp drifting and strategic boosts, you'll need precision driving to stay ahead and climb the ranks.

Mastering Drift and Boost Timing

The core loop in SCARS revolves around maintaining speed through tight corners and open straights. You'll use simple controls to steer, but the real skill lies in initiating drifts to carry momentum and timing your boosts for maximum effect. Each race demands quick reactions and a feel for the car's handling as you weave through traffic and rival racers.

As you progress, the challenge ramps up with faster opponents and more demanding circuits. Success unlocks new cars and performance upgrades, letting you tailor your ride to your driving style. Global leaderboards track your best times and race results, giving you a clear target to chase as you refine your technique and push for higher rankings.

PLAY BETTER

SCARS tips

  1. Drift into corners to maintain speed and set up a boost exit.
  2. Save boosts for long straights where they have the most impact.
  3. Learn each track's layout to anticipate sharp turns and avoid collisions.
  4. Upgrade your car's performance to stay competitive in later races.
  5. Watch rival cars' lines to find overtaking opportunities.

SCARS FAQ

What is the main objective in SCARS?

The main objective is to win high-intensity races by outpacing opponents using sharp drifting and strategic boosts. You race a compact turbo city car across urban, night, and futuristic tracks.

How do controls work in SCARS?

Controls are simple: use a mouse click or tap to play. The same action accelerates and steers, and you can initiate drifts by releasing and reapplying input around corners.

What progression systems are in SCARS?

Winning races unlocks new cars and allows you to upgrade performance. You can also climb global leaderboards by achieving faster times and better race results.
