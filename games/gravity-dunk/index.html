About Gravity Dunk

Gravity Dunk reimagines the classic flappy formula in a modern 3D setting. You control a basketball, tapping to keep it aloft while threading through a series of hoops. The goal is simple: score by passing through the center of each hoop, but one mistake ends your run.

Mastering the Hoop Line

Each successful pass through a hoop's center scores points and pushes you forward into more demanding layouts. Hoops appear at varying heights and angles, demanding precise timing and controlled taps. The challenge escalates quickly, testing your ability to judge distances and adjust your trajectory mid-flight.

Power-ups can turn the tide. Shields absorb a collision, while Slow Motion gives you extra time to navigate tight spots. Stars double your points for a limited time. With multiple ball skins to unlock, from classic orange to shimmering gold, you can personalize your look as you chase a high score on the leaderboard.

PLAY BETTER

Gravity Dunk tips

  1. Tap gently and rhythmically rather than mashing the button to maintain a stable flight path.
  2. Aim for the visual center of each hoop; grazing the rim is a run-ender.
  3. When Slow Motion appears, use it to line up your next move through a tight gap.
  4. Grab a Shield early if you see one—it can save you from a careless collision later.
  5. Experiment with different ball skins to find one that feels right for your play style.
QUICK ANSWERS

Gravity Dunk FAQ

What is the main objective in Gravity Dunk?

You control a basketball and must fly through the center of a series of hoops. Each successful pass scores points, but hitting the rim or missing a hoop ends your run.

How do I control the ball in Gravity Dunk?

On PC, press SPACE, the Up Arrow, or X to jump. On mobile, simply tap the screen. You can pause anytime with ESC or the on-screen Pause button.

What power-ups are available in Gravity Dunk?

There are three power-ups: Shields protect you from collisions, Speed Boosts (Slow Motion) give you extra time to navigate tight spots, and Stars double your points.
