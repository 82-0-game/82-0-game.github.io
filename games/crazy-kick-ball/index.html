Crazy Kick Ball

Crazy Kick Ball is a 3D soccer game where you dribble past defenders, fake the keeper, and shoot to score and beat your high score.

About Crazy Kick Ball

Crazy Kick Ball puts you on the pitch with one clear goal: get the ball past the defenders, fool the keeper, and fire it into the net. It's a fast-paced 3D football challenge that tests your timing and decision-making as you push to beat your own high score.

Reading the Defense

Your run starts with the ball at your feet. As you dribble forward, defenders close in and try to tackle you. You need to time your moves carefully—dodge left or right, find the gap, and keep control of the ball. The keeper waits at the goal, ready to block any shot that isn't well placed.

Each attempt is a fresh chance to improve your score. The challenge ramps up as you face tougher defensive setups, forcing you to read the play and react quickly. Faking the keeper is key: wait for the right moment, then shoot with precision. With many skins to unlock, you can personalize your player as you keep chasing that higher score.

PLAY BETTER

Crazy Kick Ball tips

  1. Wait for defenders to commit before changing direction.
  2. Use a quick fake to make the keeper move early.
  3. Aim for the corners of the goal for a better chance to score.
  4. Stay patient and find the gap rather than forcing a shot.

Crazy Kick Ball FAQ

How do you score in Crazy Kick Ball?

Dribble the ball past defenders, fake the keeper, and shoot into the goal. Timing your shot when the keeper is off balance gives you the best chance to score.

What happens when you get tackled?

If a defender tackles you, your run is stopped and you'll need to start again. The goal is to avoid tackles by dodging at the right moment.

Can you change your player's look?

Yes, Crazy Kick Ball offers many skins to choose. You can select a different skin to personalize your player before or during play.
