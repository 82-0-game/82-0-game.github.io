About Mini Boxing Final Round

Mini Boxing Final Round is an arcade-style boxing game where you step into the gloves of Nick Joe. Your goal is to defeat six opponents and win the mini boxing world championship. Inspired by classic arcade boxing games, it blends simple controls with strategic timing to create a challenging fight experience.

Mastering the Three-Punch Arsenal

Your success in Mini Boxing Final Round hinges on mastering three punches: the Jab, the Straight, and the super Uppercut. Each move has its own range, speed, and damage, so you'll need to choose wisely based on your opponent's position and guard. The game's arcade roots shine through in its straightforward controls, but don't be fooled—landing hits requires precise timing and positioning.

Beyond throwing punches, you must also defend and recover. Holding guard blocks incoming attacks, while dodging lets you slip past hooks and jabs. If you get knocked down, you'll need to tap the spacebar rapidly to get back up before the count ends. With six opponents to beat, each fight tests your ability to read patterns and adapt your strategy on the fly.

PLAY BETTER

Mini Boxing Final Round tips

  1. Use the Jab to interrupt your opponent's attacks and keep them at bay.
  2. Save the Uppercut for when your opponent is open or recovering from a missed punch.
  3. Mix up your timing and punch selection to avoid becoming predictable.
  4. Dodge to the side when your opponent throws a straight, then counter with a Straight of your own.
  5. If you get knocked down, tap Space as fast as you can to get up before the count ends.
QUICK ANSWERS

Mini Boxing Final Round FAQ

How many opponents are in Mini Boxing Final Round?

You must defeat six boxing fighters to win the mini boxing world championship.

What are the controls for Mini Boxing Final Round on desktop?

On desktop, use Z for Jab, X for Straight, C for Uppercut, arrow keys to move, up arrow/W to guard, down arrow/S to dodge, and Space to get up when knocked down. P or Esc pauses the game.

Is there a mobile version of Mini Boxing Final Round?

Yes, on mobile you tap the on-screen buttons to perform the same moves as on desktop.
