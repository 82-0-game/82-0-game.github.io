About CueSoccer

CueSoccer is a sports hybrid that fuses the tactical precision of billiards with the scoring rules of football. Your goal is to pot balls into the opponent's goal while defending your own, using careful aim and angle control to outmaneuver your rival.

Mastering Angles and Aim

In CueSoccer, each turn you line up your shot by clicking or tapping to set the direction and power. The cue ball acts as your striker, and you must calculate rebounds off cushions to send the target ball toward the goal. Simple mechanics hide a layer of strategic depth as you plan your next move.

Matches are surprisingly intense because every shot carries risk. A poorly judged angle can leave your opponent an easy scoring chance. You'll need to balance offensive strikes with defensive positioning, making each decision count. Whether playing solo against the AI or challenging a friend, the game rewards careful planning and precise execution.

PLAY BETTER

CueSoccer tips

  1. Aim for the center of the target ball to send it straight toward the goal.
  2. Use cushion rebounds to reach balls that are not directly in line with the goal.
  3. Play defensively by leaving the cue ball in a position that blocks your opponent's easy shots.
  4. Practice controlling shot power to avoid overshooting the goal.
  5. In two-player mode, observe your opponent's patterns and adjust your strategy.

CueSoccer FAQ

How do you score in CueSoccer?

You score by potting a ball into the opponent's goal, similar to scoring in football. The first player to reach the target number of goals wins the match.

What are the controls for CueSoccer?

The game is played entirely with the mouse or touch. Click or tap on the cue ball, drag to set your aim and power, then release to shoot.

Can you play CueSoccer with two players?

Yes, CueSoccer supports a two-player mode where you and a friend take turns shooting on the same device, competing to score the most goals.
