About Moto Bike Extreme Hill Stunts

Moto Bike Extreme Hill Stunts is a physics-based motocross racing game that challenges your precision and timing. Ride powerful stunt bikes through insane obstacle courses across mountains, factories, and floating platforms, aiming to conquer all stages with jaw-dropping flips and jumps.

Mastering the Physics

Your core loop is simple: accelerate, brake, and jump at the right moments to keep your bike balanced on tricky terrain. Each track demands careful throttle control and well-timed use of the front and back flips to land cleanly. The physics engine reacts to every input, so smooth, deliberate actions beat reckless speed.

As you progress, stages introduce steeper hills, tighter platforms, and more complex obstacles that test your ability to read the terrain ahead. Success comes from learning when to commit to a jump and how to adjust your bike mid-air. Only riders who master the balance between speed and control will finish every stage and pull off the most spectacular stunts.

PLAY BETTER

Moto Bike Extreme Hill Stunts tips

  1. Brake before steep descents to avoid losing control.
  2. Use front flips to rotate quickly over short gaps.
  3. Back flips are safer for clearing tall obstacles.
  4. Time your 360 jump only when you have enough air time to complete the rotation.
  5. Watch the terrain ahead to anticipate the next jump or obstacle.
QUICK ANSWERS

Moto Bike Extreme Hill Stunts FAQ

What is the main objective in Moto Bike Extreme Hill Stunts?

The goal is to ride your stunt bike through each obstacle course, performing flips and jumps while maintaining balance, and to conquer all stages by finishing them successfully.

How do I perform stunts in the game?

While in the air after a jump, press the Up Arrow for a front flip, Down Arrow for a back flip, or Space bar for a 360 jump. Timing is key to landing safely.

What kind of tracks can I expect?

The game features extreme tracks set in mountains, factories, and on floating platforms, each with hills, obstacles, and gaps that require precise driving and stunt execution.

GUIDEHow to play＋
Start by accelerating gently with the Right Arrow to get a feel for the bike's momentum. Use the Left Arrow to brake before sharp turns or steep drops. When you approach a ramp or hill, press Space to jump, then use the Up Arrow for a front flip or the Down Arrow for a back flip. Time your flips so you land with the wheels down. Practice on early tracks to learn how the bike reacts to different surfaces before tackling the harder stages.
