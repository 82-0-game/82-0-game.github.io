About Infini Golf

Infini Golf is a casual arcade sports game where you play an endless series of golf holes without waiting or club changes. Your goal is to aim each shot and carefully measure force to sink the ball, while occasionally collecting dollars to unlock new ball designs.

Mastering Shot Power

Each hole flows directly into the next, so you can focus on lining up your aim and judging the right amount of power. A gentle tap might leave you short, while too much force can send the ball flying past the target. The key is to read the layout and commit to a clean stroke.

As you play, dollars can appear during your shots, and grabbing them helps you unlock new balls like the coral, green, or yellow ones. With no waiting lines or club swaps, the challenge is purely about consistency and precision. Every shot counts, and the variety of balls gives you a reason to keep playing.

PLAY BETTER

Infini Golf tips

  1. Take a moment to line up your aim before committing to a shot.
  2. Use a lighter touch on short holes to avoid overshooting.
  3. When dollars appear, adjust your shot slightly to collect them without missing the hole.
  4. Unlock new balls as soon as you have enough dollars to keep the game fresh.
QUICK ANSWERS

Infini Golf FAQ

How does Infini Golf work?

You play an endless series of golf holes. After each hole, the next one starts immediately, so you just keep aiming and shooting.

What are the controls in Infini Golf?

You use a mouse click or tap to set your aim and power for each shot.

How do I unlock new balls in Infini Golf?

Collect dollars that appear during your shots. Once you have enough, you can unlock balls like the coral, green, or yellow ones.
