About Ultra Shot

Ultra Shot puts you on the court for a fast-paced basketball shooting challenge where precision and timing are everything. Your goal is to read the defender, place perfect shots into the hoop, and score under pressure. Master four distinct shot types and build streaks to earn combo bonuses.

Reading the Defender

Each possession in Ultra Shot begins with a defender in your path. You must read their positioning and choose the right shot type to beat them. Normal shots, fadeaways, power dunks, and floaters each have their own timing and effectiveness. Build streaks by scoring consecutively to earn combo bonuses that boost your score.

As you progress, you'll face three legendary boss defenders: The Wall, Lightning Hands, and the unpredictable Oracle. Each boss presents a unique challenge that tests your timing and decision-making. With three difficulty levels and increasingly intense gameplay, every shot counts. Improve your abilities in the Transfer Market to keep up with the rising pressure.

Ultra Shot tips

  1. Watch the defender's movement to anticipate their block.
  2. Mix up your shot types to keep the defender guessing.
  3. Focus on timing your release for a perfect shot.
  4. Use the Transfer Market to upgrade your abilities for tougher opponents.
  5. Build streaks early to maximize combo bonuses.

Ultra Shot FAQ

What are the four shot types in Ultra Shot?

The four shot types are normal shot, fadeaway, power dunk, and floater. Each has different timing and effectiveness against defenders.

How do combo bonuses work?

Build streaks by making consecutive shots without missing. Each successful shot increases your streak, and longer streaks earn higher combo bonuses.

Who are the legendary defenders?

The legendary defenders are The Wall, Lightning Hands, and the Oracle. Each has a unique style that challenges your timing and shot selection.
