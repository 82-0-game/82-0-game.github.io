About AIMBALL

AIMBALL is a fast-paced 3D basketball game that puts your shooting skills to the test. With just one finger, you swipe up to shoot, controlling power and aim with the length and direction of your swipe. Your goal is to score as many points as possible within a 60-second time limit.

Mastering the Shot

The core loop is simple: swipe up to shoot, with longer swipes generating more power. Tilt your swipe left or right to adjust your aim, and release to send the ball flying. You have 60 seconds to rack up points, with baskets worth 2 points, 3 from behind the arc, and 6 from special far spots that appear occasionally.

Scoring three baskets in a row ignites an ON FIRE streak, doubling your points for a limited time. When the clock hits zero, you get one final half-court shot worth 12 points. As you play, you'll earn points to unlock 29 different ball skins, from lava and lightning to nebula and hologram, and complete 20 quests to boost your score. Beat your record and share a clip or score card to prove your skills.

PLAY BETTER

AIMBALL tips

  1. Practice your swipe length to control power accurately.
  2. Aim for the far spots when they appear for 6-point baskets.
  3. Focus on hitting three in a row to trigger ON FIRE for double points.
  4. Use the final half-court shot wisely—it's worth 12 points.
  5. Complete quests to earn extra points and unlock new ball skins.
QUICK ANSWERS

AIMBALL FAQ

How do I control the power and aim in AIMBALL?

Swipe up to shoot, with longer swipes giving more power. Tilt your swipe left or right to adjust your aim. On desktop, mouse drag works the same way.

What is the ON FIRE mode in AIMBALL?

Scoring three baskets in a row triggers ON FIRE mode, which doubles your points for a limited time.

How many ball skins can I unlock in AIMBALL?

There are 29 ball skins to unlock in the Skins menu, including lava, lightning, nebula, hologram, and neon grid, plus four with a glowing aura and a spinning ring.
