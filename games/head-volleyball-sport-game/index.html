About Head Volleyball Sport Game

Head Volleyball Sport Game strips volleyball down to its essentials: pick a character, tweak the playground and weather, then face an AI opponent in a short, score-based duel. The goal is simple—outscore your rival by getting the ball to land on their side using only movement, jumps, and kicks.

Mastering the Kick and Header

Each rally starts with you controlling your chosen athlete using four buttons: two for moving left and right, one to jump, and one to strike the ball with your foot or head. Timing your jump and kick is key—hit the ball at the right moment to send it over the net with pace or place it in a corner your opponent can't reach.

Before the match, you can adjust AI difficulty and match duration, so you control the challenge. Winning requires reading your opponent's positioning and choosing whether to smash a powerful kick or use a softer header to drop the ball just over the net. As you play, you'll learn to anticipate their moves and exploit gaps in their defense.

PLAY BETTER

Head Volleyball Sport Game tips

  1. Time your jump so you meet the ball at its highest point for a more powerful kick.
  2. Use a soft header to drop the ball just over the net when your opponent is far back.
  3. Mix up your shots—alternate between powerful kicks and gentle headers to keep the AI guessing.
  4. Watch your opponent's movement and aim for the open side of the court.
QUICK ANSWERS

Head Volleyball Sport Game FAQ

How do I score points in Head Volleyball Sport Game?

You score by making the ball land on your opponent's side of the court. Use your kicks and headers to send the ball over the net and out of their reach.

What can I customize before starting a match?

You can choose your character, customize their appearance, and adjust the playground, weather, AI difficulty, and match duration.

How many buttons do I need to control my character?

You use four buttons in total: two for moving left and right, one for jumping, and one for kicking the ball with your foot or head.

GUIDEHow to play＋
Start by selecting your character and customizing their look if you wish. Then set the playground, weather, AI difficulty, and match length to your liking. Once the match begins, use the two movement buttons to position yourself under the ball. Press the jump button to leap, and time your kick button to strike the ball with your foot or head.
