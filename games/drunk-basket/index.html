About Drunk Basket

Drunk Basket is a hilarious basketball game that turns the sport into a wobbly physics playground. You control a ragdoll player whose movements are anything but steady, and your goal is to grab the ball and sink it into the hoop before your opponent does. Play solo against the AI or challenge a friend in same-device multiplayer.

Mastering the Wobbly Shot

The core loop in Drunk Basket revolves around charging your shot by holding down the designated key and releasing at the right moment to throw the ball. Your ragdoll character moves with unpredictable physics, so timing your release is only half the battle. You also need to get close to the ball to pick it up, then position yourself inside the green zone for a clean throw.

Each round introduces a random twist that changes the rules of the game, such as low gravity or a giant ball. These modifiers force you to adapt your strategy on the fly, making every match feel fresh. Whether you're facing the AI or a friend, the challenge lies in controlling your wobbly athlete and making split-second decisions under pressure.

PLAY BETTER

Drunk Basket tips

  1. Don't rush your shot; wait until you're inside the green zone for a more accurate throw.
  2. Watch for the random twist each round—low gravity means you'll need to adjust your aim higher.
  3. When playing against a friend, try to grab the ball quickly and shoot before they can set up.
  4. Practice charging your shot to find the right release point for different distances.

Drunk Basket FAQ

How does Drunk Basket work?

Drunk Basket is a ragdoll basketball game where you control a wobbly player. You grab the ball, charge your shot, and release to throw it into the basket. Each round has a random twist that changes the gameplay.

What are the controls for Drunk Basket?

Player 1 uses the W key to charge and release the shot. Player 2 uses the Up Arrow key. On mobile, use the on-screen buttons. You also need to get close to the ball to grab it.

Can I play Drunk Basket with a friend?

Yes, Drunk Basket supports two players on the same device. You can also play solo against the AI.
