About Letter Boom Blast Rush

Letter Boom Blast Rush is a demolition puzzle game where you defend your stronghold and master the Doomsday Cannon. Explore a lost treasure island by blasting letters from obstacle word cubes, clearing a path for your red stickman baseball player to reach the finish line.

Master the Doomsday Cannon

Each stage presents a unique demolition puzzle. Slide your mouse to aim the cannon and press space to fire, knocking letters off the word cubes. Clear the right letters to open a route for your runner, but watch your shots—every blast can change the board.

As you conquer stages, you can recruit powerful heroes at the Wharf Winehouse and upgrade your base to expand your empire. The game blends word puzzle strategy with arcade action, challenging your aim and planning. Unite with players worldwide to fight enemies and secure treasures.

PLAY BETTER

Letter Boom Blast Rush tips

  1. Aim for letters that are part of the word cube's core structure to collapse it efficiently.
  2. Plan your shots to clear a straight path for your runner, avoiding unnecessary blasts.
  3. Upgrade your base early to unlock better cannon capabilities for tougher stages.
  4. Recruit heroes at the Wharf Winehouse to gain advantages in later levels.

Letter Boom Blast Rush FAQ

What is the main objective in Letter Boom Blast Rush?

You blast letters from word cubes to clear a path for your red stickman baseball player to reach the finish line, while also defending your stronghold and upgrading your empire.

How do I control the cannon?

Slide your mouse to aim the cannon and press the spacebar to fire. The game uses these simple controls for all demolition actions.

What can I do to progress in the game?

Conquer demolition stages to earn rewards, then use them to upgrade your base and recruit powerful heroes at the Wharf Winehouse, which helps you tackle tougher challenges.
