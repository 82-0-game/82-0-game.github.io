About Sporty Viper

Sporty Viper puts a sporty twist on the classic snake game. Instead of food, your snake collects sports items like balls, table-tennis gear, and gloves to earn points. You also need to grab keys to unlock the next level, making each stage a fresh challenge.

Collecting and Progressing

In Sporty Viper, you guide a snake around the play area, steering it toward sports items that appear scattered about. Each item you collect adds to your score, so you'll want to be efficient and grab as many as possible. But you can't just focus on points—keys are also essential, as they unlock the path to the next level.

Every level presents its own set of challenges, so you'll need to adapt your approach. The snake's movement is continuous, so you must plan your route carefully to avoid running into walls or your own tail. With time pressure and the need to collect both items and keys, each level tests your reflexes and decision-making.

PLAY BETTER

Sporty Viper tips

  1. Prioritize keys over points when time is tight, as advancing is more important than a high score.
  2. Plan a route that lets you collect multiple items without crossing your own path.
  3. Keep the snake's tail in mind—avoid tight turns that could trap you.
  4. Use the time limit to your advantage by memorizing item and key locations for faster collection.
QUICK ANSWERS

Sporty Viper FAQ

What is the main objective in Sporty Viper?

The main objective is to collect sports items to earn points and keys to unlock the next level. Each level has its own challenges and time limit.

How do I control the snake in Sporty Viper?

You control the snake's direction using arrow keys or swipe gestures on touch devices. The snake moves continuously, so you need to plan your turns carefully.

What happens if I run out of time in a level?

If the time runs out before you collect the required keys, you'll likely fail the level and need to retry. It's important to balance collecting items with grabbing keys quickly.
