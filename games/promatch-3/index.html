About ProMatch 3

ProMatch 3 brings the excitement of sports into a match-3 puzzle. Arrange sports items like balls, bats, and accessories to create lines of three or more. With lively animations and well-paced levels, it's designed to keep sports enthusiasts engaged.

Matching Sports Gear

In ProMatch 3, your main task is to swap adjacent sports items on the board to form a line of three or more identical pieces. When you match footballs, bats, or other gear, they disappear, making room for new items to fall. The goal is to complete each level's objectives, which often involve clearing specific items or reaching a target score.

As you progress, levels introduce new challenges that require careful planning. You'll need to think ahead to set up larger matches or chain reactions. The game's smooth animations and timely level design keep the pace engaging, offering a satisfying puzzle experience for sports fans.

PLAY BETTER

ProMatch 3 tips

  1. Look for matches that can create cascades, as they clear more items in one move.
  2. Prioritize matches that align with the level's specific objectives, such as clearing certain sports items.
  3. Plan your moves to set up future matches, especially when the board is crowded.
  4. Use the entire board, not just the bottom rows, to spot hidden opportunities.

ProMatch 3 FAQ

How do I play ProMatch 3?

Swap adjacent sports items to form a line of three or more identical ones. Matching them clears them from the board, and new items fall in. Complete each level's objective to advance.

What is the main goal in ProMatch 3?

The main goal is to clear levels by matching sports items like balls, bats, and accessories. Each level has specific objectives, such as reaching a score or clearing certain items.

Are there any special items or power-ups in ProMatch 3?

The game focuses on standard match-3 mechanics. While there are no special power-ups mentioned, you can create larger matches for bigger effects.
