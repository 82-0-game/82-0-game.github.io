About Funny Volleyball

Funny Volleyball is a light and dynamic sports arcade game that puts you on a bright 3D court. Your goal is simple: outscore the computer opponent by sending the ball over the net in ways they can't return. With quick matches and simple physics, it's easy to jump in and start playing.

Mastering the Jump Timing

The core loop is straightforward: move your character around the court, jump to meet the ball at the right height, and hit it at the perfect moment to send it over the net. The ball's trajectory depends on your timing and position, so every rally feels a bit different. You'll need to watch the ball closely and react quickly to keep it in play.

As you play, you'll earn rewards and unlock new characters and balls, giving you a reason to keep coming back. The computer opponent gets tougher as you progress, so you'll need to vary your shots and anticipate their moves. Matches are short, making it easy to squeeze in a round or two whenever you have a few minutes.

PLAY BETTER

Funny Volleyball tips

  1. Position yourself under the ball early to have more time to time your jump.
  2. Watch the ball's height and your opponent's position to decide when to jump.
  3. Aim your hits to the open side of the court to make it harder for the opponent to return.
  4. Stay alert and keep moving to react quickly to fast returns.
QUICK ANSWERS

Funny Volleyball FAQ

How do I control my character in Funny Volleyball?

You use the movement buttons to move around the court and a jump button to leap. Timing your jump and hit is key to sending the ball over the net.

What is the objective in Funny Volleyball?

The goal is to score the required points before the computer opponent does. You score by hitting the ball so it lands on the opponent's side and they can't return it.

Are matches long in Funny Volleyball?

No, matches are quick. You can play a round in a couple of minutes, making it easy to enjoy short play sessions or stay longer to unlock new items.
