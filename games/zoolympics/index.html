About Zoolympics

Zoolympics is a fast, family-friendly Animal Olympics arcade game. Build a team of cartoon animal athletes—cheetah, gorilla, kangaroo, sloth, elephant and more—and mash the buttons to sprint, jump, throw, swim and lift your way to gold. Earn coins, win medals, train your roster and recruit stronger athletes.

Master the Timing Bar

In Zoolympics, each event boils down to a simple rhythm: tap the LEFT and RIGHT zones alternately to build speed and power, then hit the central action button at the right moment. The on-screen power timing bar tells you exactly when to release a throw, jump, or strike. Nail the timing window and you'll maximize your score, while mistimed taps cost you precious momentum.

As you progress, you'll earn coins and medals that let you train your existing roster and recruit stronger athletes. With 15+ events across Running, Jumps, Throws, Strength, Water and Aim, you'll need to adapt your strategy to each animal's strengths. Climb the global leaderboards and challenge rivals in the Arena to prove your team is the best.

PLAY BETTER

Zoolympics tips

  1. Alternate taps at a steady rhythm to build maximum speed without wasting energy.
  2. Watch the power timing bar closely and release just as it hits the sweet spot for a perfect score.
  3. Train your weaker athletes first to balance your team for different event types.
  4. In the Arena, study your rival's performance to adjust your strategy.
QUICK ANSWERS

Zoolympics FAQ

How does the timing bar work in Zoolympics?

The on-screen power timing bar fills as you tap the LEFT and RIGHT zones. Press the central action button when the bar is in the optimal zone to maximize your score.

What events are available in Zoolympics?

Zoolympics features 15+ events across six categories: Running, Jumps, Throws, Strength, Water and Aim.

Can I play Zoolympics on my phone?

Yes, Zoolympics plays great in the browser on mobile in landscape mode, and also on desktop with mouse and keyboard.
