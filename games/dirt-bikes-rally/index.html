About Dirt Bikes Rally

Dirt Bikes Rally puts you on a powerful stunt bike, ready to tackle a series of off-road obstacle courses. This physics-based racer demands precise timing and control as you navigate ramps, hills, and tricky terrain. Your goal is to conquer every stage by balancing speed with safe landings.

Mastering the Terrain

In Dirt Bikes Rally, each run is a test of throttle control and balance. You'll accelerate over jumps, lean into turns, and adjust your bike's angle mid-air to land cleanly. The physics engine reacts to every input, so a slight over-rotation can send you tumbling. Success comes from reading the course ahead and committing to your line.

The game rewards precision over reckless speed. As you progress, you'll face more complex obstacles that require careful planning. Beat your previous times to earn medals and unlock new bikes, each offering a different feel. The challenge is to master each stage, not just finish it, pushing your skills to the limit.

PLAY BETTER

Dirt Bikes Rally tips

  1. Use the Arrow Keys to shift your weight and keep the bike level when landing.
  2. Approach large jumps with a steady speed to avoid over-rotating.
  3. Tap Tab to maintain momentum without losing control on rough sections.
  4. Watch the terrain ahead to anticipate bumps and adjust your line early.
QUICK ANSWERS

Dirt Bikes Rally FAQ

What is the main objective in Dirt Bikes Rally?

The main objective is to ride your dirt bike through obstacle courses, performing stunts and navigating terrain to finish each stage. You aim to beat your ghost runs and collect medals.

How do I control the bike in Dirt Bikes Rally?

You use the Tab key to move forward and the Arrow Keys to move left and right. Balancing and timing your inputs is key to staying upright and landing jumps correctly.

Are there unlockable bikes in Dirt Bikes Rally?

Yes, you can unlock new bikes as you progress. Earning medals by completing stages well will help you access these new rides.
