About Kick Pong Table Soccer

Kick Pong Table Soccer is a hybrid arcade sports game that merges the classic tabletop feel of foosball with the simple, addictive rally of Pong. Your main objective is to outscore your opponent—either an AI challenger or a friend in same-device multiplayer—by swiping to hit the ball and defend your goal.

Mastering the Swipe Shot

Each match plays out on a 2D field where you control a paddle-like striker. Swipe to send the ball toward the opponent’s goal, but be ready to react quickly to defend your own. The physics are tuned to make every hit feel satisfying, whether you’re aiming for a corner or just trying to keep the rally alive.

As you play, you’ll face tougher AI in cup tournaments, where winning trophies unlocks custom kits to personalize your team. The game is easy to pick up but demands sharp reflexes and strategic shot placement to master. With matches lasting around five minutes, it’s perfect for quick duels or a longer session of escalating challenges.

PLAY BETTER

Kick Pong Table Soccer tips

  1. Time your swipes to catch the ball on the rebound for more powerful shots.
  2. Watch your opponent’s movement and aim for the opposite corner to score.
  3. Stay centered to react quickly to both attacks and defenses.
  4. In 2-player mode, mix up your shot angles to keep your friend guessing.
QUICK ANSWERS

Kick Pong Table Soccer FAQ

How do I control my striker in Kick Pong Table Soccer?

On mobile, swipe to move and hit the ball. On PC, use the arrow keys to control your striker.

Can I play against a friend?

Yes, the game features a 2-player mode that lets you battle a friend on the same device.

What is the goal of the game?

Your goal is to score more goals than your opponent by swiping the ball into their side, while defending your own goal.
