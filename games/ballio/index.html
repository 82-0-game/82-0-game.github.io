Ballio is a physics-based sports game where you launch a football toward moving gloves, losing a life if you miss.

About Ballio

In Ballio, you take on the role of a kicker aiming a football at a pair of moving gloves. The objective is simple: hit the glove to score, but miss and you lose one of your three lives. With gravity affecting every shot, timing and precision are key.

Mastering the Kick

Each attempt in Ballio starts with you positioning the football and setting your aim. As you launch, gravity pulls the ball downward, so you must account for distance and arc. The gloves move constantly, adding a layer of timing to your shot. Your goal is to make the ball touch the glove before it falls or flies past.

With only three lives, every miss brings you closer to the end. The challenge lies in reading the glove's movement and adjusting your power and angle. As you play, you'll develop a feel for the physics, turning each successful catch into a satisfying reward. It's a test of patience and precision.

PLAY BETTER

Ballio tips

  1. Aim slightly above the glove to compensate for gravity.
  2. Watch the glove's movement pattern before kicking.
  3. Use less power for closer gloves to keep the ball on target.
  4. Practice timing your shot to meet the glove mid-motion.
QUICK ANSWERS

Ballio FAQ

What happens if I miss the glove in Ballio?

If the football does not hit or touch the glove, you lose one life. You start with three lives, and losing all three ends the game.

How does gravity affect the ball in Ballio?

Gravity pulls the football downward after you launch it, so you need to aim higher for longer distances and account for the ball's arc to reach the moving glove.

What is the main objective in Ballio?

The main objective is to propel the football so it hits or touches the moving glove. Each successful hit scores, while misses cost you a life.
