GAME GUIDE

About 82-0 game

The 82-0 game is a basketball simulation where you build the ultimate NBA all-time team and see if you can go 82-0. The core action is drafting: tap a player to select them, then choose their position. Your goal is to assemble a roster strong enough to win every game in an 82-game season, testing your knowledge of player stats and team chemistry.

Drafting Your Perfect Roster

In the 82-0 game, the core loop revolves around drafting. You start with a blank slate and must select players from a pool of NBA all-time greats. Each tap adds a player to your team, and you must assign them to a specific position. The challenge is to balance star power with positional needs, ensuring you have a viable lineup.

The main challenge is managing your roster to go undefeated. With full player stats available, you must evaluate each player's strengths and weaknesses. Strategy involves prioritizing positions that are thin in talent, while also considering how players complement each other on the court. Progression comes from learning which combinations yield the best results.

What sets the 82-0 game apart is the decision-making around player selection and positioning. You can build a team of legends, but you must decide whether to stack your starting five or spread talent across the bench. The game's viral appeal stems from the addictive nature of trying different lineups to achieve that perfect 82-0 record.

How to play 82-0 game

Start with the basics

To start playing the 82-0 game, visit the website and choose the classic game option. You will be presented with a list of NBA players. Tap a player to select them, then choose their position from the options provided. Continue drafting until your roster is complete. Pay attention to player stats to make informed choices. Once your team is set, simulate the season and see if you can go 82-0. If you lose, adjust your lineup and try again.
