GAME GUIDE

About 82-0 game

82-0 game is a basketball simulation where you draft an all-time NBA team and try to finish a full season undefeated. The goal is simple: assemble a roster strong enough to go 82-0. You pick players, assign their positions, and then let the season play out, hoping your choices lead to a perfect record.

Drafting Your Perfect Roster

In 82-0 game, you start by tapping a player to select them, then choose their position. This core loop repeats as you fill out your team, balancing star power and fit. Each pick shapes your squad's chemistry and performance, making the draft the heart of the experience.

The challenge lies in building a roster that can withstand the grind of an 82-game season. You must consider player stats and how they complement each other. Strategic decisions about positions and roles are key to avoiding losses and keeping your perfect season alive.

What sets 82-0 game apart is the decision-making around player selection. You're not just picking the biggest names; you're crafting a lineup that works together. The game's viral appeal comes from this engaging, strategic layer that keeps players coming back for another draft.

How to play 82-0 game

Start with the basics

To play 82-0 game, start by tapping a player from the available pool to select them. After selecting, choose the position you want that player to play. Continue this process to build your complete NBA roster. Once your team is set, the season will simulate, and your goal is to win every game to achieve an 82-0 record. Pay attention to player stats and team balance to give yourself the best chance at a perfect season.
