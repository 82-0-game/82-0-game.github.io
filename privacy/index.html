Privacy Policy

At 82-0 game, your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our site and how we handle it. We keep things simple and transparent.

When you participate in our community features, we collect limited data. For comments, we store the displayed name you choose, the comment text itself, its moderation status, and a one-way visitor hash. This hash helps us identify repeat visitors without storing raw IP addresses. For ratings, we use an anonymous first-party cookie named af_visitor and a one-way hash. This allows us to recognize your device for rating purposes without collecting personally identifiable information. We do not store raw IP addresses through these features.

We do not run advertising on our site, so we do not share your data with ad networks or third-party advertisers. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard server logs as part of its normal operation. However, we do not have access to or control over those logs, and we do not use them for our own tracking.

We use cookies only for the ratings feature as described above. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect your ability to rate content.

We do not collect any other personal data unless you voluntarily contact us via email. If you email us at info@82-0-game.github.io, we will only use your email address to respond to your inquiry and will not add it to any marketing list.

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. If we make significant changes, we will highlight them here.

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at info@82-0-game.github.io.

This policy is an informational draft and not legal advice. For specific legal concerns, consult a qualified professional.
