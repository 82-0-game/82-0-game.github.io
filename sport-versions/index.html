Which Sport Do You Mean by 82-0?

Recognize the board you want, then read its own target. This navigator separates NBA basketball, American football, hockey, soccer and baseball without treating their roster rules as interchangeable.

🧭

The sport decides what the number means

The original 82-0 game is the five-player NBA draft. Hockey shares its eighty-two-game objective, while football, soccer and baseball use other named targets. Start with the sport you recognize, then follow its own roster rules.

🏈Which football are you looking for?

Quarterbacks and defensive fronts

Choose 20-0 Football for the NFL-style historical draft. Its perfect objective joins the regular season and playoffs; both offensive and defensive selections belong to the full roster.

Goalkeepers and midfielders

Choose 38-0 Soccer for an English top-flight XI. A draw matters to this target even when the team remains unbeaten. Formation is part of the setup.

An “82-0 game football” search leaves that distinction open. The roles above give you a quicker answer than the number alone.
