Can You Go 82-0? What the Finish Actually Proves

Eighty-two wins. No losses. That is the target in the 82-0 game. Here is how to recognize a complete result and describe a successful draft without losing the details that made it possible.

🏆The result has to be a complete 82–0

The game's perfect-season objective is a returned record of eighty-two wins and zero losses. Count both sides of the result. A full-looking roster or a strong statistical summary is still a draft until the game supplies its finish.

A completed 82–0 is evidence of success for that attempt. It supports a useful statement—this roster, in this run, reached the target. A claim that every draw or every repeat must do the same asks for much more evidence.

Practice record checker · arithmetic only

Does the written record satisfy the condition?

Use whole-number wins and losses. The opening 82–0 is a practice entry, not a run played on this page. The checker tests the total and target; it cannot calculate the result of a lineup.

82 wins and 0 losses total 82 games. This written record meets the perfect-season target; verify it against the completed game's result.

🧩Same names can describe a different draft

A successful five-player list needs more than names to be useful. A different team or period can mean different numbers for a familiar player. The next offer may also leave you with different destinations and choices.

Copying only the names discards those conditions. Reconstruct what you can: card identities, permitted placements and the actions used to reach the final roster. The player profiles help with recognition; the draft reference helps with the board.

🔬A contribution gain is not a quoted win price

The game describes a nonlinear conversion to its season record. A printed category improvement is something you can compare, but it does not come with a verified fixed number of extra wins. The exact perfect-season threshold is not established by this guide.

Use the final-pick check to explain the last choice, and the result explanation for the boundary between the statistical input and returned finish.

💬After someone reports a perfect run

Does one successful roster guarantee a repeat?

It shows that the reported attempt succeeded. It does not settle what a changed card version, different offer or repeated run will return. Keep the original conditions with the result rather than promoting one success into a universal recipe.

Does a without-Wilt run use a different success condition?

The record target remains eighty-two wins and zero losses. The additional restriction asks whether Wilt is absent from the final roster. Record both facts if that is the challenge you chose.

Can a total of 82 games still miss the target?

Yes. Wins and losses can total eighty-two while the loss count is greater than zero. Check the returned pair, not just the total or the roster's appearance.

For the 82-0 game, the most useful success claim is specific enough to review: the completed record, the five exact cards and the way the draft was built.

Keep a complete result with its roster; on the next attempt, review the decisions instead of copying names alone.
