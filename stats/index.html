82-0 Statistics: Read the Player Card

The NBA 82-0 game puts five averages on a Classic card. Start with the label you need, then use the glossary to separate its meaning from the size of the number.

🎯 PPG👐 RPG🤝 APG🖐️ SPG✋ BPG

Five labels, five different contributions

🎯

PPG · Points per game

The scoring average for the represented entry. Points from field goals and free throws feed the same scoring total. A large PPG figure tells you about volume; the card does not let you infer the shooting efficiency behind it.

Read the scoring decomposition →
👐

RPG · Rebounds per game

The individual rebounding average. It combines offensive and defensive contributions without giving their split. A stopped-play team rebound is a different kind of credit.

Inspect the rebound contribution →
🤝

APG · Assists per game

The average of credited assists. A qualifying last pass must lead directly to a made field goal; not every useful pass ends in that credit.

Follow the passing situations →
🖐️

SPG · Steals per game

The average of credited possession-winning defensive actions. An interceptor, takeaway defender or controlled deflector may receive the credit.

Open the steal-credit casebook →
✋

BPG · Blocks per game

The average of credited blocked field-goal attempts. Altering the shot and recovering the ball are separate events; BPG does not combine them.

Read the block inspection file →

📏Which kind of number are you reading?

A box-score total, a per-game card average and a roster subtotal are different quantities. The label matters as much as the digits. A stat discussion gets muddled quickly if one person means career events and another means the five draft entries.

Illustrative quantities; values are invented for this glossary
QuantityExampleWhat it means
Individual event total100 pointsPoints accumulated across the example sample
Per-game average100 ÷ 5 = 20 PPGThe total divided by five appearances
Two-card subtotal20 + 24 = 44 PPGAddition of two practice contributions in one category
Returned season recordWins–lossesThe game's output after the roster is completed

The two-card subtotal is not a newly calculated player average, a complete five-player roster, or proof that a real pair would score forty-four together. It is exactly what its label says: addition of two supplied contributions. The result screen's wins and losses belong to a separate calculation.

🔢Small decimals are not low ratings

SPG and BPG often look small beside PPG because they count different events. A value of 1.5 in a defensive category is not a score out of one hundred. Likewise, a larger printed points value does not reveal how heavily the engine weighs that category.
