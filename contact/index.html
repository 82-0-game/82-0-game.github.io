Contact 82-0 game

Have a question, suggestion, or feedback about the 82-0 basketball game? We’d love to hear from you. Whether you’re curious about how the classic draft mode works, ran into a technical issue, or just want to share your best undefeated season story, your input helps us improve the experience for everyone.

Please email us at info@82-0-game.github.io. We read every message and use your comments to refine the game and community features. If you’re reporting a problem, include as much detail as possible—such as the device and browser you’re using—so we can address it faster.

We also welcome thoughtful feedback on the community tools. Comments and ratings are moderated to keep things respectful, and we’re always open to ideas for making them more useful.

Thank you for playing and for helping build the 82-0 community. We’ll do our best to respond to your message as soon as we can.
